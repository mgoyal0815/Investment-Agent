How {{SYS}} works

  1. Each month, rank stocks that were S&P 500 members that day by 6-month strength against SPY and distance above the 200-day average.
  2. Hold 10 equal slots, split into two halves reviewed on the last trading day and on the 11th.
  3. Buy only stocks in an uptrend whose ADX trend strength is at least 25 with buyers in control, and whose on-balance volume is trending up (more volume on up days). Add half a slot to a winner up 20%, up to 3 slots.
  4. Sell when a stock drops out of the top 15. A stock up 100% or more is exempt.
  5. Do not sell on rank while the stock is above a rising 50-day average.
  6. Profit lock: once up 50%, sell if it falls 25% from its peak.
  7. Pause new buying when market breadth, high-yield credit or the large-cap index weakens. Resume buying for 42 sessions even while paused when the Coppock Curve turns up from below zero, or when cyclical sectors (discretionary, tech, industrials, financials) cross back above their 100-day average against defensive ones (staples, utilities, health care). Idle money sits in the leading index ETF, or short Treasuries while paused.
  8. When gold is leading, 4 of the 10 slots stay out of stocks.

What was tested and ruled out

  • India v8 rules on US data, and every v5 ↔ India difference.
  • 2× and 3× leveraged index ETFs: up to 58% of years, falls of 75–88%. Rejected.
  • A fixed QQQ, SPY or SMH share, indexes competing for slots, volatility targeting.
  • Re-entry signals (Coppock, McClellan, new highs minus new lows, risk-appetite ratios) with and without confirmation, and a new-low guard on them.
  • Index parking during pauses (full and partial), a giants entry path, looser profit locks, pause hysteresis, grace periods for new buys.
  • RSI, MACD, ADX, breakouts, 52-week highs, VIX, credit and breadth signals, on both the buy and sell side, across full ranges.
  • Industry momentum, a momentum-crash guard, longer momentum windows, the Nasdaq-100 universe.
  • Two Bayesian studies of 500 and 600 combinations: no enhancement helped in every period. ADX of 25 or more at entry became System F. A Coppock re-entry signal plus rising on-balance volume became System G. A cyclicals-vs-defensives restart with a 42-session window became {{SYS}}: +4.5 points of years overall and +4.1 on held-back start dates, accepted by the owner although its COVID-crash fall was slightly deeper than SPY's for a few start dates.

Verdict

No long-only system on clean price data beat SPY and QQQ every year from every start date with a smaller fall. Its losing years come from the cost of crash protection in rebounds and from a few giant companies carrying the index. {{SYS}} grows far faster than both indexes over time, and its worst fall is about SPY's. Two earlier data biases, future winners in the stock list and in the pause signals, were found and removed; every number here is after those fixes. The live system was not changed.
