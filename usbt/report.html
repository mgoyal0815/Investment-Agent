US momentum study · train 2012-19 · test 2020-26 and 2000-11 · $10,000, whole shares

Can India v8's rules make US v5 beat SPY, QQQ and SMH every year?

No. The best combination on 2012-2019 fails the 2020-2026 test. One blend, Candidate 3, earns what live v5 earns with smaller falls, but it does not beat all three indexes every year either.

The years that break every version are semiconductor and mega-cap years: SMH returned +73% in 2023 and +39% in 2024, QQQ +55% in 2023. A diversified stock book does not keep pace with that, however it ranks or sizes.

The study ran the live US v5 code, India v8 ported to US data, each of the 15 differences one at a time, and about 600 combinations. Choices were made on 2012 to 2019 only, with the search space, seed and scoring fixed in advance, then tested on January 2020 to October 2026 and on 2000 to 2011.

What changed the US system, and what did not

Candidate 3: the robust blend

The v5 stock engine with India's top-500 universe, India's buy pause instead of the regime overlay, decisions twice a month, and a sell when a holding ranks outside the top 45. Same return as v5 in 2020-26 (30.0% against 30.2%), a smaller worst fall, and 12.9% a year in 2000-11 against 1.6%. Third in training both times.

No single change helps much

Each of the 15 differences on its own moves v5 by under 2 points a year on 2012-19, and India's ranking alone makes it worse. The gains come only from combinations, which is also where overfitting comes from.

The training winner failed

Hybrid A (12 equal slots, India ranking, gold) had the best 2012-19 score: 23.9% a year, beating all three indexes in 72% of years with a −18% worst fall. On 2020-26 it fell 54% at worst and lost 20% in 2021. That gap is overfitting.

Next steps

  • Paper-test Candidate 3 beside the live v5 system. It is not what the fixed rule picked, and the 2019-26 years were seen in an earlier round, so treat it as a candidate, not a result. Nothing in the live repositories was changed.
  • A survivorship-free dataset (MarketParquet, Norgate or Sharadar) would turn 2000-2018 into a real test. The code reruns unchanged on it.
  • Beating SMH every year needs semiconductor exposure rather than better stock selection. If that is the goal, decide how much of the book should follow the leading index ETF.
