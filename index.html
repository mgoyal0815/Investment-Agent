Investment Agent

Investment Agent is a private, single-user automation. It is not a public product and it is not offered to other users.

It runs on a self-hosted n8n instance owned and operated by the author, and its only purpose is to read from and write to the author's own Google Sheets workbook, in which the author records and reviews a simulated (paper) investment research process. It places no real orders and holds no client money.
